After you submit, DJ Nova reveals its independently randomized choice.

If you chose different formats, a 50/50 draw selects between your choice and Nova’s.

Artist format: the system randomly selects one artist from the main-genre demo catalog and automatically draws that artist's songs. Subgenre format: each player supplies five eligible artists before the shared song pool is built.
